一间专业、专注的围棋教室A Dedicated, Professional Weiqi Classroom

小裴围棋课室位于新加坡淡滨尼东（Tampines Street 21, Blk 264, #01-114），由新加坡围棋协会秘书长裴令令老师创办与执教。课室目前由多位棋协业余五段老师任教，非常适合 3–99 岁的零基础与业余爱好者——无论是初次接触围棋的孩子，还是希望系统提升棋力的进阶学员，都能在这里找到合适的课程。 Located at Tampines Street 21, Blk 264, #01-114 in Singapore, Xiao Pei Weiqi Classroom was founded and is led by Ms. Pei Lingling, Secretary-General of the Singapore Weiqi Association. Our classroom is staffed by several teachers holding Chinese Amateur 5-Dan rank, and welcomes students aged 3 to 99 — from absolute beginners to advancing amateur players.

课程体系Programs

为不同阶段设计的学习路径A Path for Every Stage

启蒙Foundations

零基础启蒙Beginner Enrichment

面向3岁以上零基础学员，通过规则与基础技巧的学习，培养专注力与规则意识，激发对围棋的兴趣。For complete beginners aged 3+. Builds focus and rule-based thinking while sparking genuine interest in the game.

进阶Advancing

段位进阶与DSA规划Dan-Level Advancement & DSA Planning

系统提升棋力与逻辑思维，结合新加坡DSA（直接收生计划）升学需求，为孩子做长期规划。Systematic training in reasoning and Go skill, paired with planning support for Singapore's Direct School Admission (DSA) pathway.

业余Amateur+

业余选手提升Amateur Player Development

面向已有一定棋力的业余选手，由业余五段老师带领对局分析与实战训练，备战本地及区域赛事。For players with existing skill, led by Amateur 5-Dan instructors through game review and match practice, preparing for local and regional tournaments.
